Quick Decision Flow
  1. Cytoplasm band vs nucleus diameter → ER / MR / LR
  2. Pigment granules or Maurer's clefts? → MR or LR
  3. Minimal cytoplasm, chromatin-dominant, comma/crescent, appliqué, or double-dot → ER
  4. Perfect "textbook" ring shape → MR
  5. Dense stippling, hemozoin, amoeboid → LR

❓ Help & Shortcuts

Getting started

  1. Click 📦 Upload Dataset and choose a ZIP of images.
  2. Pick a class for the image — click a button or press its key.
  3. Pick an alternative if you were torn between two classes, or choose No Alternative.
  4. Rate the image quality: Usable, Limited or Unusable. The next image loads automatically.
  5. Click 📥 Download any time for a CSV of your labels. 📤 Load that CSV later to pick up where you left off.

Uninfected is filed as Usable in one press. Cannot Determine skips the alternative step. The visual guide and cue table are in the left sidebar.
